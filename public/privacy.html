MealJot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

MealJot (mealjot.com) is a food and nutrition tracker that runs entirely in your web browser. MealJot has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics. We never receive, see, or store your data.

Where your data lives

Your food log, recipes, and goals are plain-text markdown files kept on your device, either in your browser's storage or in a folder you choose. If you turn on cloud sync, the app also copies those files to your own cloud storage account. The copy goes directly from your browser to that provider; it does not pass through MealJot.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, MealJot asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. With it, the app can only create, read, update, and delete its own files in a hidden, app-specific area of your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. MealJot uses this access only to sync your MealJot files between your devices.

The Google sign-in token is stored in your browser and is used only by the app running in your browser. It is never sent to MealJot or anyone else. You can disconnect in MealJot's settings at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

MealJot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI models.

OneDrive

If you connect OneDrive, MealJot stores its files in the app's own folder (Apps/MealJot Food Tracker) in your OneDrive. It uses that access only to sync your MealJot files.

AI nutrition estimates (optional)

If you use "Estimate nutrition", the meal description you type is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you picked (OpenRouter, GitHub Models, OpenAI, or Anthropic), using your own account or key. That provider's privacy policy covers that request. Your Google Drive or OneDrive files are never sent to an AI provider.

Deleting your data

Clear the site's data in your browser to remove local copies. Disconnect or revoke cloud access and delete the synced files from your cloud account to remove cloud copies. MealJot holds nothing to delete on its side.
